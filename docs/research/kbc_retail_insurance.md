# KBC Belgium: retail banking and insurance offering (inventory)

Scope: private individuals at KBC Bank & Verzekering (Flanders), KBC Brussels and CBC (Wallonia). The three brands run the same product factories and near-identical apps (KBC Mobile / KBC Brussels Mobile / CBC Mobile). Research date: **30 Sep 2026**. Sources are numbered [Sx] (list at the end). Prices are included only when published, and each carries its date. "Unverified" means only a search snippet or secondary source backed it, or the page gave no date.

## Summary: what stands out

- **Personalisation is already industrialised, but only for customers who opt in.** Kate "proactively offers personalised suggestions … in more than 140 situations" [S4]. Proactive Kate needs the "Extra convenience" (*Extra gebruiksgemak*) consent. Kate Coins and tailored offers need the separate "Personalised" (*Op jouw maat*) commercial consent. MyHome and MyMobility only work with "Extra convenience" switched on [S4][S6][S11][S44]. Anything new we build would sit behind the same consent gates.
- **Kate is a lead engine, not just a chatbot.** In Q3 2025 employees picked up 656k Kate leads and 89k became sales [S4]. Kate contributes to more than 400k product sales a year [S4]. In Q2 2026 Kate triggered about 0.5M sales opportunities, with a ~19% lead-to-contract rate in Belgium (earnings call, secondary source, unverified) [S12]. Autonomy is ~82% in Belgium at end-2025 [S7] and 77% in Q2 2026 [S12].
- **Most personalised areas: daily banking and housing/mobility "ecospheres".** Examples are duplicate-payment detection, the card-abroad check, service-voucher reminders, budgets, subscriptions and savings goals [S4][S5]. MyHome (Mar 2026, 60k users by Q2 2026) and MyMobility (Nov 2025, ~390k users) add affordability checks, TCO and Kate follow-up [S6][S11][S12].
- **Insurance is broad but mostly catalogue-plus-simulator.** Most P&C and protection products can be quoted and bought in KBC Mobile/Touch, and claims (incl. video damage assessment) are digital [S28][S37][S38]. We found no public evidence of proactive coverage-gap advice or usage-based pricing. Car insurance only has mileage bands [S28]. Cross-sell happens mainly at the mortgage moment: >9/10 KBC home-loan clients also buy home insurance and >8/10 buy loan-balance cover [S7].
- **Lending is digital-first for small tickets and hybrid for mortgages.** 73% of instalment loans but only 20% of mortgage applications start digitally (2025) [S11]. Mortgages still end with a human "woonexpert" call, despite the 10–15 minute certainty flow (since 2019) [S15][S23].

**Gaps / observations**
- We found no public evidence that multicurrency, eSIM or travel insurance are suggested together when a trip is detected. The only travel trigger documented is Kate's card-activation check [S4][S8].
- Budget and subscription tools are descriptive. We found no evidence of "switch and save" nudges on subscriptions. Energy group-switching (Switch Together) sits in MyHome [S11].
- Payconiq has been replaced by Wero [S19], so P2P habits are being reset in 2025–26.
