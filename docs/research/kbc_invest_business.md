# KBC Belgium: saving and investing, private banking, self-employed, SME/agri, corporate. Offering inventory

_Research date: 2026-09-30. Scope: KBC Bank & Verzekering (Belgium). KBC Brussels and CBC (Wallonia) sell mostly the same products under their own brand names. Source tags `[Sn]`/`[Rn]` point to the list at the bottom. Most pages were read through a fetch tool that summarises them, so product names and figures were cross-checked where possible. Anything not confirmed on an official KBC page is marked **unverified**._

## Summary: what stands out

- **Advice is already digital and driven by the investor profile.** The MiFID investor profile (_beleggersprofiel_) is a digital questionnaire covering goal, risk, finances, horizon and **sustainability preferences**, plus a product-knowledge check. More than 270,000 clients have filled it in digitally. The advised product, **Strategie Plus** (Actief/Adaptief, from EUR 25/month), can be sold through the app, Kate, KBC Live or a branch. The 2025 annual report says clients "receive regular proactive proposals at appropriate times in their mobile app" and that accepting one is fully digital [S5][S6][S1].
- **Clear client tiers.** Mass retail uses the app and Kate, with remote KBC Live advisers ("Expert Vermogensopbouw", who spend half their time on outbound calls). Above that sits **Private Banking**, with a dedicated private banker (the entry threshold is not published on kbc.be). **Wealth Management** starts at EUR 5M or more. **Bolero** is the self-directed option: ETF Playlist, Invest & Repeat, and Bitcoin/Ether since 16 Feb 2026 under MiCA. **Puilaetco is not KBC**: it belongs to Quintet (Precision Capital) [S19][S13][S14][S24].
- **Self-employed: an open partner ecosystem.** It combines the Business PRO account and the Boostpack (Billit e-invoicing with Peppol, Dexxter bookkeeping, VAT activation through Acerta/Liantis), accounting-package links into KBC Mobile (Billit live; Exact, Yuki, Dexxter, Octopus and EenvoudigFactureren announced), and **Kate reminders about unpaid customer invoices**. B2B e-invoicing over Peppol has been mandatory in Belgium since 1 Jan 2026, so invoice data is now a rich, structured signal [S25][S27][S28][R11].
- **SME/agri: insight features exist, but they are mostly descriptive.** The Business Dashboard (150,000 companies) offers working-capital, CO2-footprint and sector-comparison insights. **Kate has been in the Business Dashboard since 2024** (payments, reports, insurance). Other offers include the KBC Better Building tool (EPC rules for non-residential buildings), KBC Autolease (95% of new orders were EVs in 2025) and a specialist **KBC Agro** network of about 70 staff. Relationship managers and insurance agents still lead most business sales [S26][S18][S39][S1][S35].
- **The main white space is cross-segment and planning-type proactivity.** The public material shows no automated "entrepreneur = person + company" view (pension gap across VAPZ/POZ/IPT and _pensioensparen_, company cash surplus leading to Business Invest Service, succession timing). That work is done by humans (Private Plan, Successiescan, Expert Vermogensopbouw voor ondernemers). Any proactive, personalised investment or insurance-investment nudge counts as a **personal recommendation** under MiFID II / IDD and needs a suitability assessment. Consumer credit tightens under **CCD2 from 20 Nov 2026** [S20][S21][S32][R1][R4][R6].

## Regulatory notes (relevant to personalization)

- **MiFID II suitability and appropriateness.** Advice and discretionary management need a suitability assessment (art. 25(2)). Non-advised sales of complex products need an appropriateness test (art. 25(3)). Execution-only is allowed only for non-complex products at the client's initiative (e.g., Bolero). A proactive, personalised "buy X" nudge presented as suitable counts as a **personal recommendation** (Del. Reg. 2017/565 art. 9) and needs full suitability plus a suitability report (art. 54). ESMA's 2022 guidelines (applying since Oct 2023) require collecting **sustainability preferences**. FSMA published sector reports on both assessments in 2022 [R1][R2][R3].
- **IDD.** Branch 21/23 products are insurance-based investment products (IBIPs). They need a demands-and-needs test, plus suitability/appropriateness (art. 30; Del. Reg. 2017/2359). Cross-selling rules apply (art. 24) [R4].
- **GDPR.** Profiling for marketing needs a lawful basis and respect for the **right to object** (art. 21(2)). Fully automated decisions with significant effect (e.g., credit refusal) fall under art. 22. Reusing transaction data needs a purpose-limitation check. KBC says it "lets clients decide how we use their data and how Kate can use this data" and offers a privacy overview [R5][S1].
- **Consumer credit.** The Belgian Code of Economic Law Book VII applies today. **CCD2 (Dir. 2023/2225) applies from 20 Nov 2026**, with a stricter creditworthiness assessment, advertising and information rules, and a right to human intervention in automated assessment (art. 18). For SME and self-employed credit, the **Belgian SME Financing Law (21 Dec 2013)** requires refusals to be explained transparently and caps reinvestment fees [R6][R7].
- **AI Act.** Credit scoring of natural persons and life/health insurance risk pricing are **high-risk** uses (Annex III). The 2 Aug 2026 start date may be pushed back by the EU "digital omnibus" (**unverified**) [R8].
- **Also relevant:** SFDR art. 8/9 disclosures [R10]; the EU Retail Investment Strategy (political agreement 18 Dec 2025 on inducements and value-for-money; final text/timing **unverified**) [R9]; MiCA (crypto on Bolero); the Belgian 10% capital gains tax (withheld at source since 1 Jun 2026) [S15]; Peppol B2B e-invoicing mandate [R11].
